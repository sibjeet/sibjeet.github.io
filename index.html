Hi, I'm Sib

Since 2018, I've spent most of my time as co-founder and chief product officer of Branch, a venture-backed startup that makes office furniture, work products and ergonomic tools for people and teams. We've helped half a million people and thousands of teams across North America create workspaces that improve productivity and well-being without breaking the bank. Regular side quests include writing, investing, teaching and reading as much as I can. I live in Brooklyn.

Before Branch, I helped start new ventures at Redfin, co-founded a community for pre-idea founders figuring out what's next, traveled the world as a digital nomad, and wrote a (drawer) novel about AI and post-scarcity, among other adventures. I graduated from Yale with a BA in Ethics, Politics and Economics.

More about me

Branch

Why build a new office furniture company? We interact with our desk, chair and office accessories for more hours a day than most products in our lives. Beyond the value of thoughtful design in objects we use every day, choosing the right chair and desk—and using them the right way—has a tremendous impact on our well-being.

Our mission at Branch has two phases. The first phase is launching a complete line of inspiring and ergonomic office products to make a great day of work accessible to people and teams of all sizes and budgets. The second phase is launching physical and digital experiences that work together to redefine how our offices can support productivity and well-being at work. We're well into phase two today.

Day to day, I focus on consumer growth, digital products, and connected products, including our ecommerce site, B2B platform, mobile app, and a slate of exciting new products to be announced.
